See trainings and workshops What is Hakomi?

We are building a community for exploring the habits that separate us from ourselves and others.

To recover our innate healing power, and cultivate lives that are more fully alive, joyful and connected.

We draw on the Hakomi Method, developed by Ron Kurtz, and its roots in the Feldenkrais Method, created by Moshe Feldenkrais, both integrating them and providing Teachings and Trainings to experience the depth of each method on their own.

We do not seek quick-fixes nor forceful change, but rather, to slow down, listen more deeply and connect with ourselves and others in an embodied, trusting, resourced and committed way, creating home in our similarities and differences.

We believe it is from this space that healing and thriving emerge, and also the potential for collective change.

We honor and are deeply grateful to the Hopi People, whose language gave birth to the name of the Method we teach. We are committed to reverence the word Hakomi and its meaning and to use it, respectfully and ethically, staying in relation with the culture that brought it to our world and in constant inquiry on how to do that best.

Who can benefit from our services?

Are you a therapist?

Surface unconscious core material and co-create healing experiences with your clients through deepening somatic tracking skills and attunement beyond the verbal. These gentle yet profound methods cultivate particular ways to direct your attention to find inspiration and nourishment in your clients, for authentic connections and as an antidote to burnout.

Are you a teacher or helping professional?

Relationships are at the heart of our work and you can learn to listen beyond words, cultivate an authentic, safe, resourced and attuned presence, how to stay engaged, curious and learning, and read non verbal cues to respond most efficiently and timely even in the most challenging of situations.

Are you a bodyworker?

Learn to support your students or clients ethically and safely by providing the proper container for their experience, beyond movement and touch. Heighten your awareness of their processes and develop resources to refine your ability to co-regulate and prevent dis-regulation.

We do offer trainings that fulfill the requirements for certification as a Hakomi Practitioner and a Hakomi Teacher or Trainer with the Hakomi Education Network. We do not offer psychotherapy trainings nor do we offer licenses for therapeutic practice. We also offer Advanced Trainings for both Feldenkrais and Hakomi Certified Practitioners, or Psychotherapists of different modalities. They are not certifications or licenses; you will be bound by the requirements of your state or country to practice.
