Yerina Rock

Certified Hakomi practitioner

I have been a certified Hakomi practitioner since 2020, with roots in the community stretching back to 2015. This practice continues to inspire and delight me with its ability to create healing spaces that offer full-bodied nourishment and transformation.

I was one of the founders of Hakomi Norway, and I have since been a co-creator of Hakomi Cascadia alongside Hakomi Trainer Maria Serrano and practitioner Alia Raad. Currently, I serve on the Hakomi Cascadia Council where we nurture and guide this growing community.

I am presently practicing as a psychotherapist in California*, integrating my background as a social anthropologist and my past experience as a yoga and meditation teacher into my therapeutic work.

You can learn more about me and my work at yerinarocktherapy.com.

Please note that due to licensing restrictions, I am only able to work with clients based in California.

Alia Raad

Clinical psychologist, mindfulness guide and breathwork specialist

My name is Alia and I am a clinical psychologist, mindfulness guide and breathwork specialist. I founded The Space in Between with the goal of making a positive impact on the mental health of individuals and families.

I am trained and experienced in working with a wide range of mental health issues. My areas of practice include bereavement and grief, anxiety, depression, relationship difficulties and parental management, providing guidance and support for parents looking to raise encouraged and emotionally resilient children. I also work with individuals looking to learn more about themselves and their purpose.

Over the last decade, I have had extensive experience working with adults who have lost their sense of purpose. This transformational work, standing alongside my clients as they rediscover their true selves, has enhanced my passion for the Hakomi way.

Working with clients one-on-one, I began to notice the power of intentional breathing. No matter what, the thing that will always bring an instantaneous and momentary sense of relief is taking 3 deep breaths. The fusion of breathwork and therapy has an everlasting impact that clients can carry with them anywhere. As a certified breathwork specialist, I lead Pranayama sessions, where we explore healing through mindful attention to breath, extending into somatic meditation and spiritual exploration.

Alastair Stewart

Certified Hakomi therapist

I'm interested in how we embody experience, as everything we are finds expression through our body. To be present to our felt sense, or put another way, to feel our body as we relate opens the door to our implicit sense of the world. This has a significant bearing to how we make sense of the world and our capacity for more freedom and agency in our everyday experience. Ron Kurtz, the originator of Hakomi, phrased this: "how we unconsciously organise our experience holds the key to our core beliefs and biases."

My studies in Hakomi began in 2002 with the first cohort of trainings that Donna Martin undertook in the UK. It was a rich experience with the training being undertaken over 6 day periods, giving plenty of time for deep material to surface. I found Hakomi to be a practice that reveals the obstacles to a deeper connection with our self and our clients.

My initial trainings in psychotherapy were heavily leant towards Rogers' theory of growth. As a trainee I was impressed with Eugene Gendlin's work. Along the way I qualified to supervise through a dynamic integrative teacher who had studied with Carl Rogers at La Jolla. In 2009 I was certified as a Hakomi therapist, on Donna and Flint Sparks' blessing. Since then I have studied IFS and taken short courses in the field of trauma.

Over the past 30 years I have had the opportunity to support a wonderful array of humanity, some more complex than others! Ranging from the ages of 4 to 84… I also teach Tai Chi and Qi Gong as great grounding practices. I have 2 grown up children who have helped me in the world of developmental psychology!

Brandon Wong

Hakomi and Family Constellations practitioner

I am blessed that Hakomi landed on my doorstep one day. It made me curious about myself in a very safe way and all the healing happened to me without an agenda, or solution or something wrong with me. Fast forward to now, I enjoy seeing my clients unfold safely with me being a witness to such beautiful moments.

I find Hakomi an amazing modality to use on its own, or we could integrate it into any other modality. As a Family Constellations practitioner, I practice both modalities now and find them very different yet very complementary to each other. Hakomi to me is horizontal and expansive and we can grow to be very mindful of our present moment experiences, realizing how a lot of suffering is unnecessary. Family Constellations to me is vertical and transgenerational and we look at how suffering can be entanglements with our ancestors. When I integrate these two modalities, the spark is amazing. Hakomi just makes all healing modalities safer and more gentle.

Teresa García Hubard

Senior Hakomi Trainer, Mexico City, Mexico

Teresa is a therapist, teacher, author and speaker. Since her first encounter with Hakomi over a decade ago, she felt that she had come home and hasn't ceased to be marveled by this practice. Today Teresa is a senior Hakomi Trainer based in Mexico City, Mexico.

Teresa has been a practicing clinical psychologist for over three decades, specialized in psychotherapy for children and teens, as well as the relationship between mothers and their babies. She is well grounded in psychoanalysis having studied with the Mexican Association of Psychoanalysis; University of Paris XII, French Embassy and the Franco-Mexican Association of Psychiatry. Her bachelors and masters in Clinical Psychology are from Ibero and UNAM.

Teresa is the author of the Spanish-language book No Hay Niño Malo. 12 Mitos Sobre la Infancia (There are no Bad Kids. 12 Myths about Childhood) and co-author of Despertando Tu Amor Para Recibir Tu Bebé: Cómo Prevenir la Tristeza y la Depresión en el Embarazo y Después del Parto (Awakening Your Love to Receive your Baby: How to Prevent Sadness and Depression during Pregnancy and After Birth).
